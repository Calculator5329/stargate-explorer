# Prometheus references, 2026-09-06

Ethan sent four reference images of the show's deep-space carrier in chat
(they cannot be saved from the chat surface, so this is the written record;
`ships/prometheus-def.ts` pass 6 is built from it).

What the four frames show, in order of how much they drive the model:

1. **One long flat rectangular hull**, gunmetal grey, roughly 2.5:1 length to
   full beam, flat top and bottom, near-vertical sides, a blunt prow. No
   fighter-like taper anywhere; the silhouette is a brick with details.
2. **Two large open hangar bays at the bow flanks.** Each is a rectangular box
   about a third of the beam wide, mouth facing forward, running from the bow
   back to roughly midships. They are the widest part of the ship.
3. **A tall stepped command tower amidships-aft** on a low raised deck, with
   several antenna masts on top and sensor vanes off its sides. It is the only
   tall feature; everything else stays low.
4. **Angular plating and greeble strips** along every flank, and **blocky stern
   engine housings**: a wide engine wall the full beam with rectangular
   exhausts in a row.

Colour: one mid grey with darker panel lines; no insignia; no colour accents
beyond the exhaust glow and the lights inside the hangar mouths.
