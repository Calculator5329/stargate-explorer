# SG-1 episode and fleet references — 2026-09-11

Research for the graphics pass: Antarctica is the centerpiece; more distinct ship silhouettes are the immediate outcome. This is a reference shortlist, **not an approved mission order or implemented campaign**. Objectives below are proposed game adaptations. No external models, textures, screenshots, or copyrighted production assets were downloaded into the repository.

## Source quality and limits

- **Primary production reference:** producer/writer Joseph Mallozzi's [Ships of Stargate: SG-1 collection](https://josephmallozzi.com/2018/03/12/march-12-2018-ships-of-stargate-sg-1/), which he describes as production ship concepts and designs, many by James Robbins. His [Prometheus follow-up](https://josephmallozzi.com/2018/03/13/march-13-2018-the-prometheus-in-depth-and-a-few-stargate-related-links/) is the specific reference hub for that ship. Text retrieval verified the pages and provenance but did not expose the gallery images for geometric inspection.
- **Primary production concept lead:** Mallozzi's [February 12, 2018 collection](https://josephmallozzi.com/2018/02/12/february-12-2018-food-for-thought-and-assorted-goodies/) explicitly labels James Robbins's Anubis mothership, Tagrean fighter, and Tagrean dirigible concepts. Search indexing exposed this description; direct page retrieval failed. Treat this as a source lead, not proof of a particular final screen silhouette.
- **Episode and craft corroboration:** the linked episode guides and lexicon are on [Richard Dean Anderson's official website](https://www.rdanderson.com/stargate/episodes/index.htm). They are useful participant-site editorial references, not production scripts or independently inspected episode footage. The table's episode events were checked against those guides. Source names should not imply that Anderson personally authored every entry.
- A publicly indexed archival *Camelot* script was found, but PDF retrieval failed. It was not used as evidence. No exact ship dimensions, fleet counts beyond the four arriving Ori ships, technical performance, or production measurements are asserted here.
- Silhouette construction suggestions are **art-direction inferences**, not measured proportions. Any new model that blends classes should be labeled “inspired by” until compared against final screen references. Production concepts can differ from screen versions.

## Antarctica composition recommendation

Build the recognizable geography and hierarchy first: flat ice, a clear horizon, cargo ship close to the surface, Prometheus overhead, fighters crossing beneath it, larger hostile silhouettes at altitude. This is an art recommendation derived from the verified battle participants, not a shot-for-shot reconstruction.

The new cargo ship provides the visual reason for the battle. The drilling shaft gives the scene a fixed landmark. The gold drones provide the release. A decorative icy planet alone would not make the playable scene read as Antarctica. Keep the showcase labeled **scripted graphics preview** until actual objectives and combat behavior exist.

For a review sheet, use Antarctica, the Ori supergate, the *Fallen* hull pass and the Replicator/Asgard confrontation as four distinct visual compositions. They test ice terrain, negative space in giant hulls, close surface detail, and faction silhouette separation without committing to campaign order.
